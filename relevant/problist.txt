[Application Logic]
The practice mode will now use a "Weighted Random Selection" algorithm. 
Instead of every question having a 1/N chance, questions in High Yield chapters 
will have their "tickets" multiplied by their weightage factor.
Example: A Calculus question is ~2.5x more likely to appear than a Sequence question.
